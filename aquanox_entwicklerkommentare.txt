    (Z10, [Fog]) Alpha-Value of the input TGA file.

    (Z22, [Fog]) This value between 0 and 1 indicates, at which proportin of the
    (Z23, [Fog]) complete viewing distance the fog starts getting opaque
    (Z26, [Fog]) The maximal alpha, that should be reached at the closing barrier.
